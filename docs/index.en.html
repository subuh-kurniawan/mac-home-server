🏡 Philosophy & Personal Story

Technology with a Heartbeat: Distance Is Just a Number When Home Is Always Listening

Working hundreds of miles away from family demands an authentic way to stay present without always being physically in the room. Conventional chat apps reduce long-distance family connection into cold notifications on small screens—messages frequently buried under work group chats, or calls left unanswered while children are playing and mothers are busy in the kitchen without a phone in hand. There is a deep, primal longing to genuinely greet the living room: encouraging children during morning breakfast, keeping daily prayer and meal schedules alive, and radiating parental presence directly into the heart of the home.

📢

Acoustic Presence over Screens

Synthesized chime bells and gentle Indonesian TTS announcements in the living room speaker create a tangible physical presence. Children don't need a phone in their hands to hear their father's love.

🔒

Zero-Trust Sovereign Privacy

Zero commercial cloud subscriptions, zero data leaks. All personal messages, audio recordings, and family routines are encrypted end-to-end through a private WireGuard VPN tunnel.

🍃

Second Life for Hardware

A quiet Mac mini running 24/7 with only ~6 Watts of idle power. Far from obsolete, it orchestrates Google Cast, Android TV, and native CoreAudio into one harmonious ecosystem.

“Ultimately, this project isn't about code architecture or latency benchmarks. It is about a father's love crossing hundreds of miles of highway—ensuring that no matter the physical distance, his family at home always feels heard, protected, and accompanied every single day.”

Modular Architecture

Pro-Grade Capabilities in Liquid Glass

Engineered around Apple design principles: precision, elegance, and uncompromised utility.

⚡

Quick Action Broadcast & Auto-Restore

Instant voice broadcast engine targeting Google Home with synthetic harmonic chimes (Intro: E5-G5-C6, Outro: G5-E5-C5). Automatically elevates speaker volume to 70% during announcements, then seamlessly restores it to baseline once finished.

📢

Static-First Cast

Casting speeds under 1ms with zero Wi-Fi subnet scanning delays. Instant TLS socket connectivity to Google Home and Xiaomi TV Stick.

Latency: < 0.8 ms
🤖

Telegram Remote Bot

Command your server from anywhere via @Subuh_macBot. Capture desktop screenshots (/screen), monitor processes, and stream Voice Notes.

🧠

Assistant Cloud SDK

Dispatch text commands to Google Assistant programmatically without physical mics listening. Spoken responses automatically stream to Google Home.

🎮

Android TV Remote v2

Control TV Stick Mi over TLS Port 6466. Virtual D-Pad navigation, power/volume toggles, and deep-link shortcuts to streaming apps.

📁

Kodbox-Style Web Explorer

Browse and manage Mac mini files from your phone or laptop with bufferless Range 206 video streaming, multi-file uploads, and ZIP extraction.

🛡️

WireGuard VPN & Zero-Trust

Access your server remotely without exposing ports on your home Wi-Fi router. End-to-end encrypted with custom X.509 TLS/SSL certificates.

Quickstart

Installation & Setup

Get your Mac mini up and running as a home command center in 3 simple steps.

1. Clone & Install Python Libraries

git clone https://github.com/subuh-kurniawan/mac-home-server.git
cd mac-home-server
pip3 install pychromecast google-assistant-grpc google-auth-oauthlib "cryptography<45" androidtvremote2

2. Copy Config Templates & Generate SSL

cp bot_config.example.json bot_config.json
cp cast_devices.example.json cast_devices.json
cp tv_remote_paired.example.json tv_remote_paired.json

# Generate Local Self-Signed SSL Certificate (Port 8443 HTTPS)
openssl req -x509 -newkey rsa:2048 -nodes -keyout key.pem -out cert.pem -days 365 -subj "/CN=localhost"

3. Launch Server & Telegram Bot 24/7

# Run HTTPS Dashboard & Telegram Bot Controller
python3 server.py &
python3 telegram_bot.py &

Open your browser and navigate to https://localhost:8443.
